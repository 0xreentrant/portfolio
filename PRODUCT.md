# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Visitors evaluating Alexander Perez as a person and collaborator: prospective clients, peers, and people who found him via LinkedIn or iknowai and want the human context before any product pitch. Context: curious, time-poor, allergic to founder theater.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio for Alexander Perez (Denver). First job of the site is biography and presence - who he is, how he thinks, how to reach him - not a catalog of products. Product work ships later as secondary surfaces. Success: a stranger understands the engineer-who-teaches posture in one scroll and can contact him without friction.

## Brand Personality

Warm expert: patient, specific, never salesy. Engineer credibility without jargon. Family-scale practice energy (dinner with Brenda matters). Three words: empathetic, credible, practical.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS hero metrics, identical card grids, purple gradients
- Tech-bro AI hype and dark neon futurism
- Course-guru scarcity theater and countdown CTAs
- Product-first portfolios that lead with app mockups before the person
- Cream/sand editorial monoculture and display-serif drop-cap magazine pastiche

## Design Principles

1. **Person before product** - Biography and voice lead; shipped work is optional later.
2. **Name the stance** - Engineer who teaches, not coach who bluffs.
3. **Restraint with intent** - Spatino-informed monochrome and type hierarchy, not blank "minimal."
4. **Specificity over slogans** - Denver, bilingual EN/ES, real career arc; no abstract futurist claims.
5. **Reachable** - Contact is a first-class surface, not a footer afterthought.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast for body text. Semantic landmarks, keyboard-reachable nav, meaningful alt text on portrait. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. No motion-gated content.
